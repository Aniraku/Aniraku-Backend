# Copyright and DMCA Policy

**Effective date: October 2026**

Aniraku respects the intellectual-property rights of others and expects everyone using the service or this repository to do the same. Aniraku-Backend is the service layer of the Aniraku project: it does not host video files, operate streaming servers, or store infringing material, and this repository contains only source code. Streams, metadata, and artwork are supplied by third-party providers and services. Where valid copyright notices identify material reachable through the service or present in this repository, Aniraku will act expeditiously to remove or disable access to it, consistent with the U.S. Digital Millennium Copyright Act (17 U.S.C. § 512) and other applicable law.

## Designated copyright agent

Copyright notices, counter-notices, and related correspondence should be sent to the designated agent:

**Aniraku Copyright Agent: [dmca@aniraku.tech](mailto:dmca@aniraku.tech)**

Include "DMCA Notice" or "DMCA Counter-Notice" in the subject line so the message is routed without delay.

## Filing a takedown notice

Under 17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3), a valid notice must be in writing and include **all** of the following:

| `REQUIRED ELEMENT` | `DETAIL` |
| --- | --- |
| Signature | A physical or electronic signature of a person authorized to act for the owner of the allegedly infringed right. |
| Work identified | Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to be infringed, or a representative list where multiple works are covered. |
| Material located | Identification of the challenged material plus information reasonably sufficient to locate it. For repository content: the exact file path, with branch or commit. For service content: the exact route, title, episode, or URL as served by the backend. Screenshots alone are not sufficient. |
| Contact details | The notifier's name, organization where relevant, mailing address, telephone number, and email address. |
| Good-faith statement | A statement that the complaining party has a good-faith belief the disputed use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law. |
| Accuracy statement | A statement, made under penalty of perjury, that the information in the notice is accurate and that the complaining party is authorized to act on behalf of the owner of the allegedly infringed right. |

Notices missing any element cannot be processed as DMCA notices and will be returned with an explanation of what is needed.

## What happens after a valid notice

1. The notice is reviewed for completeness and validity.
2. For service content: access to the identified material through Aniraku is removed or disabled expeditiously. This may mean delisting a title, route, or provider path rather than deleting anything, since Aniraku does not host the underlying files. For repository content: the flagged file or excerpt is removed or edited, and the change is published in the repository.
3. Where the material was supplied by a user (for example a comment or attached item), the affected user is informed where contact details exist.
4. Repeat-infringer accounts are terminated (see below).

## Filing a counter-notice

If you believe material was removed or disabled through Aniraku as a result of mistake or misidentification, you may submit a counter-notice to the agent above. Under 17 U.S.C. § 512(g)(3), it must include:

1. Your physical or electronic signature.
2. Identification of the removed material and where it appeared before removal.
3. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that you have a good-faith belief the material was removed or disabled as a result of mistake or misidentification.
4. Your name, address, and telephone number, plus a statement consenting to the jurisdiction of the U.S. Federal District Court for your district (or any such court if you are outside the United States), and that you will accept service of process from the original notifier.

On receiving a valid counter-notice, Aniraku forwards it to the original notifier. Access may be restored 10 to 14 business days later unless the original notifier confirms court action seeking to restrain the material.

## Repeat-infringer policy

Consistent with 17 U.S.C. § 512(i), Aniraku terminates, in appropriate circumstances, the accounts of users who are repeat infringers, including users who repeatedly post infringing comments, repeatedly land their content in valid notices, or repeatedly trigger valid notices against material they control.

## Misrepresentations

Knowingly making a false claim in a notice or counter-notice can create liability for damages under 17 U.S.C. § 512(f), including costs and attorney's fees. Send notices only for material you genuinely believe infringes a right you own or are authorized to enforce.

## Scope and good faith

Because Aniraku aggregates third-party sources, some remedies available to a hosting service (such as deleting a stored file) do not apply to service routes; the effective remedy is removing or disabling the route or code path by which the material is reached, and where appropriate raising the notice with the upstream provider. This repository is expected to stay free of media files, player bundles, and credential material, and contributions are reviewed with that in mind. This policy is a statement of practice, not legal advice. It may be revised as the service or applicable law changes.
